HOW TO READ CONSTRUCTION TIMING

Verified records, not projected starts

Three different kinds of timing information are kept separate to help salespeople prioritize responsibly.

Official permit dates

Issued, applied, inspected, and expiration dates appear only where municipal source fields supply them.

Inspection evidence

A last-inspection date means an inspection record exists. It does not confirm site progress, the work phase, or whether the project is still active.

Suggested sales timing

Contact windows are based solely on days since issuance. They are not predicted groundbreakings, construction schedules or completion dates.
