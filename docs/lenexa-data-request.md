Subject: Request for public Lenexa building-permit issuance CSV/API

Hello,

I am researching new residential and commercial construction activity in Lenexa and would like to use the City’s public permit information for market analysis and business development.

I found your monthly Building Permit Reports and the Accela Citizen Access permit portal. Is there a city-approved CSV, Excel report, or documented public API for individual building permits issued in the most recent 12 months, ideally with weekly or monthly refreshes?

Useful public fields would include the issuing jurisdiction, permit number, issuance date, public status, street address, type/work class (especially new houses, duplexes, townhomes, multifamily, new commercial buildings and structural additions), brief work description, valuation, square footage or unit count if public, publicly listed builder/contractor COMPANY if releasable, and an official permit-detail or search URL.

I am not asking for protected personal information, access credentials, private plans, or data behind non-public portals. If the City provides this through a public records request instead, please let me know the preferred process, formats, and any fees.

Thank you for your assistance.
